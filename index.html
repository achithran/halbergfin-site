Questions
Everything You Need to Know
Do I need prior trading experience to join any course?
+
No. Our Beginner to Investor course starts from absolute zero. The other courses include foundational refresher modules too. Everyone from complete beginners to experienced traders finds value here.
How are the classes conducted — online or offline?
+
All courses are conducted online — a combination of live Zoom sessions and recorded video lessons. You can attend live from anywhere in the world and access recordings immediately after each session.
What is the fee for each course?
+
Please WhatsApp or call us for current pricing. We offer different fee structures and EMI options. We also provide bundle discounts for students enrolling in multiple courses.
How long does each course take to complete?
+
Beginner (24 hrs), Forex (40 hrs), Options (35 hrs), Psychology (50 hrs). With 2–3 sessions per week, completion takes 2–4 months. All sessions are recorded so you can catch up if you miss one.
Will I get a certificate after completing the course?
+
Yes. Every course comes with a Halberg Fin completion certificate on passing the final assessment. The Trading Psychology course includes a lifetime certificate shareable on LinkedIn.
What is the TradeGuard system mentioned in the Psychology course?
+
TradeGuard is our proprietary emotional trading control platform. It monitors your emotional state through check-ins and trade patterns, and automatically blocks impulsive orders when your emotion score is too high. Psychology course students get 3 months free access — worth ₹15,000.
Can I join if I'm an experienced trader who is struggling with consistency?
+
Absolutely — in fact this is our most common student profile. Experienced traders who are technically sound but emotionally inconsistent find our Trading Psychology course the most transformative. The emotion gate system alone changes everything.
How do I contact my mentor directly?
+
Every enrolled student is added to a private WhatsApp group with their course mentor. You can ask questions, share live trades, and get real-time feedback directly. No bots, no waiting, no support tickets.
